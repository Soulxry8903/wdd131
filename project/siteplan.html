My First Pet

A practical, reassuring guide for people welcoming a pet for the first time.

Site name

My First Pet

The name is direct and welcoming. It tells new pet owners that the site is for the beginning of their experience and is easy to remember.

Site purpose

My First Pet gives first-time pet owners a clear place to begin. It offers practical preparation steps, a starter checklist, and prompts to discuss individual care needs with a veterinary professional. The site is designed to help visitors feel organized without replacing professional advice.

Audience scenarios

  • What should I prepare at home before bringing my first pet home?
  • How can I keep track of the supplies and appointments I need to arrange?
  • What questions should I bring to my pet’s first veterinary visit?

Color scheme

The palette uses calm greens and warm neutrals to make the guide feel approachable. Dark green is used for actions and emphasis; cream is the main page background; pale green marks supportive sections; warm peach adds a small accent.

  • Deep green · #25332EHeadings and primary text
  • Forest green · #345D4CButtons, links, and emphasis
  • Warm cream · #FBFAF6Main page background
  • Soft green · #EAF0E2Checklist and supportive sections
  • Warm peach · #F2A78CSmall decorative accents

Typography

Manrope is used for headings to give page titles a clear, friendly presence. DM Sans is used for body copy, navigation, and form controls for comfortable reading at small sizes. Both use a sans-serif fallback if the web fonts cannot load.
